# Hrithik Kedia - Personal Website

Build a premium, modern, highly personal portfolio website for Hrithik Kedia.

This should NOT feel like a traditional corporate portfolio or an online resume. It should feel like the digital home of a generalist operator, business builder, and community builder—someone who has spent his career stepping into ambiguous situations, figuring things out quickly, and taking ownership across functions.

CORE PERSONAL BRAND

Name: Hrithik Kedia

Primary positioning:
Generalist Operator | Founder’s Office | Business Builder | Community Builder

Core narrative:
I don't define my career by one function.

I've worked across business development, strategy, operations, finance, marketing, growth, category ownership, partnerships, customer experience, and special projects—usually wherever the business needed someone to take ownership and figure things out.

My career has been a series of experiments in learning how businesses actually work.

Today, I operate in the Founder’s Office ecosystem and currently lead business responsibilities at Primus Senior Living, while also co-building the Founder’s Office Club (FOC), a community for generalists, Chiefs of Staff, EIRs, and Office-of-CxO professionals.

The central idea of the website should be:

"I build where the problems are messy and the playbook doesn't exist yet."

Alternative supporting line:
Strategy is interesting. Execution is where I feel at home.

DESIGN DIRECTION

Take inspiration from the feel of a thoughtfully designed personal profile website: clean, editorial, modern, and premium.

However, make this website distinctly personal and not overly corporate.

Visual personality:

Minimal but warm

Modern editorial design

Confident without feeling flashy

High-quality typography

Generous whitespace

Subtle motion and interactions

A mix of professional credibility and human personality

Avoid:

Generic startup gradients everywhere

Overly animated tech-bro aesthetic

Resume templates

Skill progress bars

Buzzword-heavy design

Too many cards or dashboards

Suggested aesthetic:

Use a sophisticated neutral palette—off-white/cream backgrounds, charcoal/dark text, with one subtle accent colour. The site should feel like something a great operator or investor would enjoy browsing.

Use large typography for important statements and smaller editorial copy for context.

IMPORTANT COPY & TONE GUIDELINES

The writing should sound like Hrithik, not like a corporate bio.

Tone:

Conversational

Self-aware

Thoughtful

High ownership

Curious

Slightly witty where appropriate

Never arrogant or overly self-congratulatory

Avoid phrases like:

"Results-driven professional"

"Dynamic leader"

"Passionate self-starter"

"Proven track record of excellence"

Generic corporate jargon

Instead, use storytelling and specific moments to demonstrate capability.

The website should make someone think:

"This person has seen a lot of different parts of a business, learns ridiculously fast, and would probably be useful in a room where nobody knows exactly what to do next."

The goal is to build a personal brand website that can be shared with founders, investors, operators, potential employers and the broader startup ecosystem—and immediately communicate:

Who Hrithik is, how he thinks, what he has built, and why he is different from someone with a conventional functional career.
